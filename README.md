My PCB is inspired by my favorite character: Kirby :D. Functionally, it is a simple circuit powered by a coin cell battery that lights up two red LEDs positioned right on his face to simulate his cute, blushing cheeks. It also includes two resistors to make sure the electricity flows safely so the LEDs don't burn out.

Materials List
1 x Battery (BT1): CR2032 3V coin cell battery.

2 x LEDs (D1 & D2): Red Light Emitting Diodes (for Kirby's cheeks).

2 x Resistors (R1 & R2): 220-ohm (220Ω) surface-mount resistors.
